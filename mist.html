The Adjective "Specific"

"Specific" in physical quantities is now used to mean "per unit mass". Hence "specific gravity" should now be called by its other name "relative density." "Speceific heat" now means "per unit mass" and the concept hitherto called "specific heat" needs to be called "specific heat capacity".

"Latent heat" now means any amount of heat involved in a change of state for whatever mass is being considered. The msin concept hitherto called "latent heat" now needs to be called "specific latentheat".
